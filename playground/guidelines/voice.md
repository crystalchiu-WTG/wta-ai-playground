## 2. How we sound

> **FILL IN**: where on the tone spectrum does your team sit? Pick one or write your own:
>
> - **Professional but warm.** Clear, credible, friendly without being chatty.
> - **Formal and authoritative.** Polished, precise, expert.
> - **Playful and conversational.** Warm, occasionally witty, contractions are fine.

Tests for tone:

- Would a senior reader roll their eyes? Cut the line.
- Would a brand-new user feel patronised? Reword.
